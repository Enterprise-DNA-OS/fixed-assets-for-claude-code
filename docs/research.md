## Product and import evidence

- [FMIS product](https://www.fmis.co.uk/solutions/fixed-asset-management-software/): financial registers, depreciation, books and reporting, with optional modules and implementation scoped to the buyer.
- [FMIS supplier service description](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/974282591266722): core asset records, lifecycle events and CSV/spreadsheet export through the product's reporting tools. It does not specify universal export headings. The importer therefore requires an explicit heading map.
- [NZ IAS 16](https://standards.xrb.govt.nz/standards-navigator/nz-ias-16/): cited review and disposal principles. The free base supplies record checks, not accounting certification or a tax engine.

The base is a financial-book opening register with straight-line depreciation and evidence workflows. It is not a claim to duplicate all FMIS methods, revaluation, physical scanning, integration or regulatory outputs. The ten questions show working analyses, not unsupported claims about the incumbent's reporting limits.
